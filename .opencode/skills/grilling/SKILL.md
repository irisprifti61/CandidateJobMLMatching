# Grilling

Interview the user until the important decisions, assumptions, constraints, and tradeoffs are explicit.

- Ask one question at a time and wait for the answer before continuing.
- Resolve prerequisite decisions before dependent ones.
- Include a recommended answer and its main tradeoff with each question.
- Investigate facts through available sources instead of asking the user to retrieve them.
- Keep decisions with the user; do not silently choose on their behalf.
- Do not implement the idea until the user confirms that the shared understanding is sufficient.
