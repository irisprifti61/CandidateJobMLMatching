# Research

1. Define the question, decision it informs, and evidence needed.
2. Prefer primary sources: official documentation, specifications, papers, source code, and first-party datasets.
3. Separate verified facts, interpretations, and unresolved questions.
4. Check dates, versions, licensing, and source provenance where they affect conclusions.
5. Record findings where the repository already keeps research. If no convention exists, propose a location before creating a new documentation structure.
6. Cite every material claim with a stable link or repository path.

Research is complete when another engineer can reproduce the reasoning and make the intended decision without repeating the investigation.
